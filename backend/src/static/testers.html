How it works

  • Join live prediction pools in the Whistle app.
  • Participants earn points based on prediction accuracy.
  • The participant(s) with the highest total points between their best two games for the week win.
  • Tie policy: If multiple participants are tied for the highest score, the weekly prize is split evenly among them.
  • Prize: A total of $50 USD per week will be awarded during the Test Phase.
  • No purchase necessary. Participation is free.
